Little Loaf Bakery

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Little Loaf Bakery is a private tool for running one bakery. It has no server. Nothing it records is sent to us, because there is no “us” to send it to — the bakery’s records stay on the bakery’s own devices and in the bakery’s own Google Drive.

Who this is for

The app is used by the owner and staff of a single bakery to take orders, track stock and send invoices. It is not a customer-facing app, and it is not distributed publicly.

What the app stores

All of this is entered by the bakery, on the bakery’s own device:

KindWhat
CustomersName, phone number, delivery addresses, and an optional map link for an address
OrdersItems, weights, flavours, prices, delivery dates, notes and status
MoneyPayments, discounts, delivery charges and invoices
StockMaterials, quantities and what they cost
TechnicalAn identifier for each installation, used to keep devices in sync

Where it is kept

  • On the device, in the app’s private storage, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. Other apps on the phone cannot read it.
  • In the bakery’s own Google Drive, if Drive sync is switched on. This is how two devices — say a kitchen phone and a counter tablet — show the same orders, and it is also the backup.

What we access in Google Drive

The app requests a single permission, drive.file. That permission grants access only to files the app itself created. The app creates one folder, named “Little Loaf Bakery”, and works inside it.

Everything else in the Google account — documents, photos, other folders — is invisible to the app. It is not that we choose not to look; the permission does not allow it.

The app does not request, and cannot obtain, access to Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other Google service.

What leaves the device

Only two things, and both go where the bakery sends them:

  • To the bakery’s Google Drive — the records above, so that the bakery’s own devices stay in step and the data survives a lost phone.
  • To WhatsApp, Maps or a dialler, when a person taps a button — the app opens the other app with a message or an address already filled in. It never sends anything by itself; the person taps Send in WhatsApp.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. No third party receives this data. It is never sold, and there is nobody to sell it to.

Location

The app does not read the device’s location. Where a delivery address has a map link, that link was pasted or picked by the bakery when the address was saved, and it is stored with the address and deleted with it.

How long it is kept

For as long as the bakery keeps it. Records stay until they are deleted in the app. Backups in Drive are kept for the last fourteen days, and older ones are removed automatically.

Deleting everything

  • On a device: uninstall the app. Its private storage, and the encryption key with it, are removed by Android.
  • In Drive: delete the “Little Loaf Bakery” folder, and remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because there is no server, that is genuinely all of it. Nothing is left behind anywhere else.

Children

The app is a business tool for bakery staff and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
