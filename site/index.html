Little Loaf Bakery

Order book, kitchen board and stock ledger for one bakery

A private Android app for running a small bakery: taking orders, keeping track of what is due when, and sending the customer a message about it.

What it does

  • Takes orders with their own delivery date, time and address per item
  • Shows the kitchen what is due today, and what is in the oven
  • Records payments, issues an invoice, and messages it over WhatsApp
  • Tracks materials and what they cost
  • Keeps two devices in step through the bakery’s own Google Drive
No server, and no account with us. Everything lives on the bakery’s own devices and in the bakery’s own Google Drive. The app asks for one Google permission — drive.file — which reaches only the folder it created itself, and nothing else in the account.

Who it is for

The owner and staff of one bakery. It is not on the Play Store and is not offered to the public.
