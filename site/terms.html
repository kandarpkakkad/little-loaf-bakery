Terms of Service

Last updated 20 September 2026

Little Loaf Bakery is a private tool, built for and used by one bakery. It is not sold, not subscribed to, and not offered to the public. These terms say what it is and what it is not, in plain words.

Who may use it

The owner and staff of Little Loaf Bakery, on devices the bakery controls. The app is not published on any store and is not made available to anyone else.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no fee, no subscription and no account to buy. There is also no paid support and no service level: it is a tool the bakery runs for itself.

The bakery’s records belong to the bakery

Everything entered into the app — customers, orders, payments, stock — belongs to the bakery. It is held on the bakery’s own devices and, if sync is switched on, in the bakery’s own Google Drive. Nobody else receives a copy. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

Keeping the account safe

The app is only as private as the Google account and the devices it runs on. Keeping those secure — a screen lock, a password nobody else has — is the bakery’s responsibility. The app can lock itself behind the device PIN or a fingerprint, and switching that on is recommended for any device that leaves the counter.

Backups

The app keeps a nightly backup in the bakery’s Google Drive and keeps the last fourteen. That is a real backup and it is tested, but it is not a guarantee: Drive is Google’s service, not ours, and a backup that has never been restored is a hope rather than a plan. Restoring one occasionally is worth the ten minutes.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is. It is written carefully and covered by an automated test suite that runs on every change, but no software is free of faults. It is not a substitute for the bakery’s own records where those are required by law — tax records in particular.

To the extent the law allows, no liability is accepted for loss or damage arising from using it, including lost data.

Other services it opens

The app can hand a message to WhatsApp, an address to a maps app, or a number to the phone’s dialler. Those are separate services with their own terms, and what happens once a message leaves for one of them is between the bakery and that service.

Changes

These terms may change as the app does. The date at the top changes with them.
